# **XTRAVON STUDIO**

## **Product Requirements Document (PRD)**

### **1\. Product Overview**

**Xtravon Studio** is a digital fashion studio that allows individuals and organisations to create, customise, consult on, order, and track the production of custom-made fashion.

The platform is designed to bring the experience of a physical fashion studio into a convenient digital environment while maintaining the human expertise of professional fashion designers and consultants.

Customers should be able to move from an initial idea or inspiration to a completed garment through one connected journey:

**Inspiration → Design → Customisation → Measurements → Consultation → Approval → Production → Fitting → Quality Check → Delivery**

Xtravon Studio should make customers feel involved throughout the entire process rather than simply placing an order and waiting for it to be completed.

---

# **2\. Product Vision**

To create a modern digital fashion studio where anyone can transform an idea, inspiration or requirement into a professionally designed and personally fitted garment while remaining connected to the fashion professional throughout the entire journey.

---

# **3\. Product Goals**

Xtravon Studio should:

1. Allow customers to create completely customised fashion designs.  
2. Allow customers to begin with templates or inspiration.  
3. Allow customers to upload images for design inspiration.  
4. Provide professional fashion consultation.  
5. Allow customers to communicate with fashion consultants.  
6. Allow customers to book live consultations.  
7. Allow customers to manage their measurements.  
8. Allow customers to maintain multiple measurement profiles.  
9. Provide a clear design approval process.  
10. Give customers visibility into the production process.  
11. Allow customers to receive production updates and images.  
12. Allow controlled changes to orders during production.  
13. Provide a structured fitting and adjustment process.  
14. Support individual and organisational customers.  
15. Provide administrators with control over major studio services and workflows.  
16. Create a clear record of communication, approvals, changes and production progress.

---

# **4\. Target Customers**

## **4.1 Individual Customers**

Individuals who want:

* Custom clothing.  
* Personalised fashion.  
* Wedding and event outfits.  
* Traditional/native wear.  
* Corporate or smart clothing.  
* Special occasion clothing.  
* Unique designs.  
* Professional fashion advice.

## **4.2 Organisations**

Organisations that require:

* Staff uniforms.  
* Corporate clothing.  
* Event clothing.  
* School or institutional clothing.  
* Group outfits.  
* Branded clothing.  
* Large-volume custom orders.

The organisation experience should support multiple people under one order rather than treating every garment as an entirely separate customer journey.

---

# **5\. Core Customer Experience**

The central experience of Xtravon Studio should be built around a **Project/Order Journey**.

Each customer's fashion project should have a visible status from beginning to completion.

### **Proposed journey**

1. Create a design  
2. Select inspiration or template  
3. Customise design  
4. Add fabric and colour preferences  
5. Add measurements  
6. Request consultation if desired  
7. Consultant reviews design  
8. Customer receives final design proposal  
9. Customer approves design  
10. Price and production details confirmed  
11. Production begins  
12. Customer tracks progress  
13. Fitting takes place  
14. Adjustments are made where necessary  
15. Quality check  
16. Outfit becomes ready  
17. Customer chooses collection or delivery  
18. Order completed

---

# **6\. Design Studio**

The **Design Studio** is the heart of Xtravon Studio.

Customers should have multiple ways to begin a design.

### **Customers can:**

* Start from scratch.  
* Select an existing fashion template.  
* Upload an inspiration image.  
* Describe what they want.  
* Combine elements from different designs.  
* Customise colours.  
* Select fabrics.  
* Adjust garment styles.  
* Select sleeve styles.  
* Select neckline styles.  
* Adjust garment length.  
* Specify special design requirements.  
* Add written instructions.

The customer should not need professional fashion knowledge to use the Design Studio.

The experience should use simple language and visual choices wherever possible.

---

# **7\. Design Assistance**

Xtravon Studio should provide guided design assistance.

For example, if a customer selects a particular garment style, the platform can suggest appropriate options and explain the choices in simple language.

The customer can either:

**Design independently**

or

**Get professional assistance.**

### **Admin control**

The administrator should be able to turn **Design Assistance** on or off from the administration area.

This allows Xtravon Studio to introduce or temporarily suspend the service without changing the customer's entire design experience.

---

# **8\. Fashion Consultation**

Consultation should be optional.

Customers should be able to choose:

### **Chat Consultation**

The customer communicates directly with a fashion consultant.

### **Live Consultation**

The customer books a consultation with a fashion consultant.

### **No Consultation**

Customers who already know what they want can proceed without professional consultation.

---

# **9\. Consultant Experience**

Fashion consultants should have access to the customer's design project so that they can understand the customer's requirements before giving advice.

A consultant should be able to review:

* Customer's design.  
* Inspiration images.  
* Measurements.  
* Fabric preferences.  
* Colour choices.  
* Special instructions.  
* Previous discussions.  
* Customer requests.  
* Previous changes.

The consultant should be able to provide recommendations and request clarification where necessary.

The consultation should become part of the customer's overall fashion project rather than existing separately from the order.

---

# **10\. Consultation Administration**

Administrators should be able to control:

* Whether consultations are available.  
* Whether chat consultations are available.  
* Whether live consultations are available.  
* Consultant availability.  
* Consultation pricing where applicable.  
* Consultation duration.  
* Which consultants handle particular categories of fashion.  
* Whether customers can request a specific consultant.

---

# **11\. Measurements**

Customers should have two primary options.

### **Option 1 — Customer-provided measurements**

Customers can enter their measurements themselves through a guided process.

### **Option 2 — Professionally verified measurements**

A customer can have their measurements taken or verified by a Xtravon Studio consultant.

---

# **12\. Measurement Profiles**

Customers should be able to save multiple measurement profiles.

Examples:

* My Measurements  
* Spouse  
* Child 1  
* Child 2  
* Client 1  
* Client 2

A saved profile can be selected when creating a new fashion project.

Customers should also be able to update their measurements when necessary.

---

# **13\. Final Design Review**

Before production begins, every custom order should pass through a final review.

The consultant reviews the design and confirms that the important requirements have been properly captured.

The customer then receives a complete order summary containing:

* Final design.  
* Fabric.  
* Colour.  
* Measurements.  
* Special instructions.  
* Agreed price.  
* Production timeline.  
* Fitting requirements.  
* Delivery or collection arrangements.

The customer must then give explicit final approval.

### **Primary action:**

**APPROVE & START PRODUCTION**

This creates a clear point at which the customer confirms what Xtravon Studio is expected to produce.

---

# **14\. Pricing and Quotation**

Before final approval, customers should understand the expected cost.

The quotation should clearly identify relevant components such as:

* Design cost, where applicable.  
* Consultation cost, where applicable.  
* Fabric.  
* Labour.  
* Special design requirements.  
* Alterations.  
* Delivery.  
* Other applicable charges.

If a change later affects the price, the customer should receive an updated quotation before the change is implemented.

---

# **15\. Production Tracking**

Production tracking should be one of the defining features of Xtravon Studio.

Customers should see a visual production journey.

### **Proposed production stages**

**✓ Design Submitted**

**✓ Consultation Completed**

**✓ Measurements Confirmed**

**✓ Fabric Confirmed**

**✓ Design Approved**

**✓ Production Started**

**● Pattern Preparation**

**○ Cutting**

**○ Sewing**

**○ Fitting**

**○ Adjustments**

**○ Finishing**

**○ Quality Check**

**○ Ready for Collection/Delivery**

**○ Delivered**

The current stage should be clearly highlighted.

---

# **16\. Production Updates**

Xtravon Studio should allow the studio to provide customers with progress updates.

Updates may include:

* Written progress notes.  
* Photographs.  
* Fitting notices.  
* Requests for customer decisions.  
* Requests for clarification.  
* Production milestone notifications.

For example:

> **Production Update**

> Your outfit has moved to the sewing stage. The initial fitting will be scheduled once the garment is ready.

This makes the customer feel connected to the production process.

---

# **17\. Change Requests**

Customers should be able to request changes during production, but changes should be controlled.

### **Customer process**

**Request Change → Studio Review → Approval/Quotation → Customer Confirmation → Production Continues**

Not every production stage should permit changes.

For example:

* Design stage — changes freely permitted.  
* Before cutting — changes may be permitted.  
* After cutting — changes may involve additional cost.  
* During fitting — adjustments permitted.  
* Final finishing — changes may be restricted.

The studio should determine whether a requested change is possible.

---

# **18\. Fitting Experience**

When the outfit reaches the fitting stage, the customer should receive a notification.

The customer should have two options:

### **Physical Fitting**

Visit the studio for professional fitting.

### **Remote Fitting Feedback**

For customers who cannot visit the studio, they may provide fitting feedback remotely.

The customer can describe the issue and provide supporting information where appropriate.

The fashion consultant reviews the feedback and confirms the required adjustment.

---

# **19\. Fitting Approval**

After adjustments, the customer should be able to confirm whether the garment meets the agreed requirements.

Where another adjustment is required, the project returns to the appropriate adjustment stage.

The goal is to avoid declaring an outfit complete until the fitting process has been satisfactorily addressed.

---

# **20\. Quality Control**

Before an outfit becomes ready for collection or delivery, it should pass through a studio quality check.

The quality check should consider:

* Design conformity.  
* Measurements.  
* Stitching.  
* Finishing.  
* Fabric condition.  
* Requested details.  
* Customer-approved specifications.  
* General presentation.

Only an approved garment should move to:

**READY FOR COLLECTION/DELIVERY**

---

# **21\. Collection and Delivery**

Customers should be able to choose between:

### **Studio Collection**

The customer collects the completed outfit from the studio.

### **Delivery**

The studio arranges delivery to the customer's chosen destination.

Customers should be able to see the delivery/collection status as part of their order journey.

---

# **22\. Notifications**

Customers should receive important notifications throughout their project.

Examples include:

* Consultation confirmed.

* Design approved.  
* Production started.  
* Production milestone reached.

For example:

Creates designs, consults, approves, pays and tracks orders.

Reviews designs, advises customers, conducts consultations and assists with fitting.

Reviews completed garments before delivery.

Manages the overall studio, customers, consultants, orders, production and settings.

Manages an organisation's group or corporate orders.

* Number of designs created.

* Consultation bookings.  
* Completed orders.  
* Repeat customers.  
* Customer satisfaction.  
* Average production time.  
* Number of successful fittings.

# **42\. Final Product Vision**

Xtravon Studio should feel like having a **fashion studio in your pocket**.

A customer should be able to wake up with an idea, create the concept, receive professional guidance, confirm their measurements, approve the final design, watch the garment being made, participate in the fitting process and ultimately receive the finished outfit.

The experience should combine the convenience of digital technology with the personal relationship traditionally associated with a trusted fashion designer.
